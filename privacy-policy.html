Monolith Miner — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026 | Publisher: Fantest Apps

Overview

Monolith Miner is a single-player Android idle mining game. You do not need an account or an internet connection for ordinary gameplay. The version currently published on Google Play is ad-free and does not offer purchases using real money. Optional rewarded-video advertising is being tested in separate preview builds and may become available in a future Play update. Watching a rewarded ad is always voluntary; ordinary mining and upgrades do not require advertising.

Data used by the game

Mining progress, currency, relics, achievements, offline earnings, and game settings are saved locally in the app's private storage on your Android device. The game uses this information to restore your progress and remember your preferences. Fantest Apps does not collect this data from your device or operate a server that receives game progress.

Data collection and sharing

Monolith Miner does not request your name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, or account credentials for gameplay. Fantest Apps does not operate a server that receives your saved game progress. The version currently published on Google Play does not include advertising or developer-operated analytics. If a future update enables optional ads, Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect or receive technical and advertising data as described below; therefore, this policy does not claim that advertising versions collect or share no data.

Optional rewarded advertising (preview and future versions)

Some preview builds, and possibly a future published version, integrate the Google Mobile Ads SDK to offer an optional rewarded video from the in-game Supply Terminal. The SDK may process and share the device IP address (which can indicate approximate location), app interactions such as video views and taps, diagnostic/performance data, and device or account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID or app set ID. Google may use such data for ad delivery and measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. Advertising requests are transmitted using secure connections. In regions where required, the app requests advertising consent using Google's User Messaging Platform and provides an option to review privacy choices when applicable.

See Google's Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures. You can also manage your device's advertising identifier and applicable ad-personalization preferences in Android settings. Skipping a video does not stop gameplay; the in-game reward is issued only if the ad SDK reports it as earned.

Android platform services

The app may participate in Android's optional device backup feature, depending on your device and Google account backup settings. Android may back up locally stored app data to your personal backup account; this is controlled by the Android operating system and your account settings, not by a Monolith Miner developer-operated service. Google Play and your device platform may separately process technical information under their own privacy policies.

Device features

Monolith Miner uses optional haptic feedback, which can be disabled in settings. The current ad-free Google Play version does not require an internet connection for gameplay. Preview and future versions supporting optional rewarded videos need internet access to load them, but do not require precise location permission.

Data deletion

Use the in-game reset option to delete local gameplay progress, or clear app storage or uninstall. Android backup copies are managed through your device and Google account backup settings. If you have viewed optional ads, resetting local game progress does not erase records processed separately by Google; use Google privacy and advertising controls to manage those records and preferences.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy if the game's functionality changes. The effective date at the top will be updated. For privacy questions, please contact Fantest Apps through the project's public support issue tracker. Do not post personal information in a public issue.
