Monolith Miner — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026 | Publisher: Fantest Apps

Overview

Monolith Miner is a single-player Android idle mining game. The game does not require an account or an internet connection to play. The current app version does not display advertising or offer purchases using real money.

Data used by the game

Mining progress, currency, relics, achievements, offline earnings, and game settings are saved locally in the app's private storage on your Android device. The game uses this information to restore your progress and remember your preferences. Fantest Apps does not collect this data from your device or operate a server that receives game progress.

Data collection and sharing

The current version of Monolith Miner does not ask for your name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, or account credentials. It does not include advertising, tracking, or developer-operated analytics. Fantest Apps does not collect, sell, or share personal data through the game.

Android platform services

The app may participate in Android's optional device backup feature, depending on your device and Google account backup settings. Android may back up locally stored app data to your personal backup account; this is controlled by the Android operating system and your account settings, not by a Monolith Miner developer-operated service. Google Play and your device platform may separately process technical information under their own privacy policies.

Device features

Monolith Miner uses the device's vibration feature for optional haptic feedback. Haptics can be disabled in the game's settings. The game does not request internet or location permissions.

Data deletion

Use the in-game reset option to delete saved gameplay progress from the app. You can also clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Android backup copies, if any, are managed through your device and Google account backup settings.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy if the game's functionality changes. The effective date at the top will be updated. For privacy questions, please contact Fantest Apps through the project's public support issue tracker. Do not post personal information in a public issue.
